# Telegram Stars CPA Tasks Bot

> Earn Telegram Stars and TON for completing simple tasks — subscribe to channels, boost posts and get rewarded. A Telegram-first CPA task exchange with a referral program and a partner API.

[![Telegram](https://img.shields.io/badge/Telegram-Open%20Bot-2CA5E0?logo=telegram&logoColor=white)](https://t.me/StarsCashFlowbot)

## What it is

**StarsCashFlow** is a **Telegram CPA (cost-per-action) task exchange**. Two sides meet inside one bot:

- **Workers** complete tasks — subscribe to channels, boost posts — and earn **Telegram Stars** and **TON**.
- **Advertisers** fund campaigns and get real subscribers and engagement for their channels.

It is built for anyone who wants to **earn Telegram Stars for free tasks**, and for **channel owners and marketers** who want affordable, targeted promotion.

## Key features

- **Earn Stars and TON** — get rewarded for completing simple subscription and boost tasks.
- **Telegram-first** — everything runs inside Telegram; no separate app required.
- **Advertiser campaigns** — fund a budget with Telegram Stars and create channel-growth tasks.
- **Automatic verification** — subscription is checked automatically via the Telegram API.
- **Two-level referral program** — earn 10% from your referrals and 3% from their referrals.
- **Built-in balance and withdrawals** — internal balance with a withdrawal request flow.
- **Multi-language** — Russian and English interface.
- **Web app and storefront** — Telegram Mini App, public services market and order history pages.
- **Partner API** — a public API for integrations.

## How it works

### For workers (task earners)

1. Open the bot and press `/start`.
2. Pick a task and subscribe to the target channel.
3. Press "Check" — the bot verifies your subscription automatically.
4. Stars are credited to your internal balance.
5. Request a withdrawal once you have earned enough.

### For advertisers

1. Switch to advertiser mode.
2. Top up your balance with Telegram Stars.
3. Add the bot as an admin to your channel.
4. Create a task: choose the channel, the reward and the number of subscribers you want.
5. The budget is reserved and your task goes live for workers.

### Referral program

- **Level 1:** 10% of your referral's earnings.
- **Level 2:** 3% of your referral's referral's earnings.
- Referral link format: `t.me/StarsCashFlowbot?start=ref_<your_id>`
