# FAQ

**How do I earn?**
Open the bot, pick a task, subscribe to the target channel, and press "Check". Telegram Stars are credited to your balance after automatic verification.

**What can I earn?**
Telegram Stars and TON.

**How is my subscription verified?**
Automatically, through the Telegram API — the bot checks that you are a member of the target channel.

**How do I withdraw?**
Submit a withdrawal request from the bot once you have earned enough; it is then processed by the operator.

**How does the referral program work?**
You earn 10% of your direct referrals' earnings and 3% of their referrals' earnings. Share your link `t.me/StarsCashFlowbot?start=ref_<your_id>`.

**I'm a channel owner — how do I get subscribers?**
Switch to advertiser mode, top up with Telegram Stars, add the bot as a channel admin, and create a task.

**Is there an API?**
Yes — a public partner API at `https://api-stars.ros.media`. See the [API reference](../API.md).

**What languages are supported?**
Russian and English.
