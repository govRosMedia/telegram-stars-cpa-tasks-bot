# Features

## Earn Telegram Stars and TON

Complete simple tasks — channel subscriptions and post boosts — and get rewarded in Telegram Stars and TON.

## Automatic verification

When you press "Check", the bot verifies your channel subscription automatically through the Telegram API. No manual proof needed.

## Advertiser campaigns

Advertisers fund a budget with Telegram Stars and create channel-growth tasks, choosing the reward and the number of subscribers they want.

## Two-level referral program

- Level 1: 10% of your direct referrals' earnings.
- Level 2: 3% of their referrals' earnings.

## Internal balance and withdrawals

Earnings accumulate on an internal balance. Workers submit withdrawal requests that are processed by the operator.

## Multi-language

The bot interface is available in Russian and English.

## Web app and storefront

A Telegram Mini App, a public services market and an order history page complement the chat interface.

## Partner API

A public API (`POST /api/v2`) lets partners integrate the task exchange into their own systems. See the [API reference](../API.md).
