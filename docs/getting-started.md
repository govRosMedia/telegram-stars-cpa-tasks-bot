# Getting Started

## Earn as a worker

1. Open [@StarsCashFlowbot](https://t.me/StarsCashFlowbot) and press `/start`.
2. Browse the available tasks.
3. Pick a task and subscribe to the target channel.
4. Press "Check" — the bot verifies your subscription automatically through the Telegram API.
5. Telegram Stars are credited to your internal balance.
6. When you have earned enough, submit a withdrawal request.

## Promote as an advertiser

1. Switch the bot to advertiser mode.
2. Top up your balance with Telegram Stars.
3. Add the bot as an administrator to your channel.
4. Create a task: choose the channel, the reward per action, and the target number of subscribers.
5. Your budget is reserved and the task becomes visible to workers.

## Use your referral link

Share your referral link in the format `t.me/StarsCashFlowbot?start=ref_<your_id>`.

- You earn **10%** of what your direct referrals earn.
- You earn **3%** of what their referrals earn.

## Explore the web app

The bot also offers a Telegram Mini App, a public services market and an order history page. Open them from the bot menu.
